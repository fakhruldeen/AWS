# Lecture 01: AI/ML Fundamentals & The AWS Ecosystem

## ⏱️ Timing & Delivery Strategy
- **Duration:** 90 Minutes
- **Lecture Slide Deck:** `slides/Module_01_AI_and_GenAI_Foundations_Slides.md` (Part 1)

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Define the Machine Learning lifecycle: Data Preparation, Feature Engineering, Model Training, Evaluation, and Deployment.
2. Differentiate between AWS AI managed services (Amazon Rekognition, Polly, Transcribe, Comprehend, Textract) and Amazon SageMaker AI.
3. Master SageMaker tools: **SageMaker Canvas** (No-code ML), **Data Wrangler** (Visual data cleaning), and **Feature Store**.

## 🗣️ Instructor Talking Points
- Emphasize that Cloud Practitioner tests *what* cloud is, while AI Practitioner tests *how* models are applied, evaluated, and governed.
- Walk students through SageMaker Canvas: business analysts can build ML models without writing a single line of Python.
