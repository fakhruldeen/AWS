# Lecture 02: Generative AI & Amazon Bedrock

## ⏱️ Timing & Delivery Strategy
- **Duration:** 90 Minutes
- **Lecture Slide Deck:** `slides/Module_01_AI_and_GenAI_Foundations_Slides.md` (Part 2)
- **Hands-On Transition:** `labs/Lab_01_Amazon_Bedrock_Playground_and_Prompt_Engineering.md`

## 🎯 Learning Objectives
1. Understand the Amazon Bedrock value proposition: Serverless access to leading Foundation Models via a single unified API.
2. Compare Foundation Model families: Anthropic Claude, Meta Llama, AI21 Jurassic, Cohere Command, Stability AI SDXL, and Amazon Titan.
3. Master model customization spectrum: Prompt Engineering $\rightarrow$ Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) $\rightarrow$ Fine-Tuning $\rightarrow$ Continued Pre-training.

## 🗣️ Instructor Talking Points
- Emphasize the data privacy guarantee of Amazon Bedrock: *AWS never uses customer input prompts or completions to train the underlying base foundation models, and data is encrypted in transit and at rest.*
