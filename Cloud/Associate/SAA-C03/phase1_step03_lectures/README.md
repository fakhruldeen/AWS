## Instructor Resources & Pedagogy
- **Timeline & Delivery Outlines:** Strict time-boxed minute-by-minute classroom structures.
- **Whiteboard Prompts:** Clear architectural diagrams to sketch on physical or digital whiteboards.
- **Exam Traps & Anti-Patterns:** Dedicated callout sections highlighting common distractor choices and candidate misconceptions.
- **Hands-on Lab Alignments:** Step-by-step handoffs to the guided labs in `labs/` and diagnostic quizzes in `knowledge_checks/`.
