### Question 232

**Domain:** `Domain 4: Billing, Pricing, and Support` | **Topic:** `Billing and pricing` | **ID:** `q0986`

> **Scenario:**  
> How can AWS enable a company to control expenses as an application's usage changes unpredictably?

#### Options
- **A.** AWS will refund the cost difference if a customer moves to larger servers.
- **B.** The application can be built to scale up or down automatically as resources are needed **[CORRECT]**
- **C.** Spot instances will automatically be used if the price is lower than on-demand instances.
- **D.** Amazon CloudWatch will automatically predict what resources are needed.

**Correct Answer:** `B`

#### Comprehensive Explanations

> Correct answer: The application can be built to scale up or down automatically as resources are needed. This item belongs to the Billing, Pricing, and Support domain, under Billing and pricing.

- **Option A** ❌ (INCORRECT): Evaluation of option A.
- **Option B** ✅ (CORRECT): Correct.
- **Option C** ❌ (INCORRECT): On-Demand Instances — pay by the second with no commitment, best for unpredictable workloads and short-term testing.
- **Option D** ❌ (INCORRECT): Amazon CloudWatch — collects metrics, logs and alarms describing how resources and applications are performing, and can trigger actions when a threshold is breached.
